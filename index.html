About me

I'm an Electrical Engineering Technology graduate from the Northern Alberta Institute of Technology (December 2025, Dean's Honor List), eligible for ASET C.E.T. certification and based in the Edmonton / Airdrie area.

My training is broad and hands-on: protective relay testing and commissioning, medium-voltage apparatus diagnostics, Allen-Bradley PLC and VFD programming, SCADA & HMI development, industrial networking, and electrical design using AutoCAD, ASPEN, Revit, and ETAP.

I enjoy bridging the gap between design and field work – from drafting a full IFC drawing package and running load-flow analysis, to commissioning live equipment and building real-time monitoring systems. I'm looking to grow with a team where I can apply this range across power, automation, and design.
